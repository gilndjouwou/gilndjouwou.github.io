### AD Clean is a professional cleaning company based in Orléans, France. They provide high-end event cleaning for trade shows, exhibitions, and public or automotive events in France and neighboring countries. Their services cover full-site cleaning, waste sorting and removal, sanitary maintenance, stand and aisle cleaning, plus vehicle cleaning and on-demand cleaning kits for exhibitors.
